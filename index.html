FYP Diary Housekeeper

A private Telegram bot for one university final-year-project group.

The group keeps a shared diary for its project: links to posts and articles, short written reflections, and a short vlog from each member every two weeks. The bot files all of it into the group's own Google Drive folder, names every file to one standard, keeps an index spreadsheet, and sends reminders before each deadline.

Who can use it

Only the members of the group, listed by the group's admins. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.

Google access

The bot uses the Google Drive, Docs and Sheets APIs with the group owner's own Google account, to create folders, upload files, and keep the index sheet and journal documents in the group's Drive folder. It does not access anyone else's Google data.
